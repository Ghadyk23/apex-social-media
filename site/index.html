About APEX

Trusted by word of mouth, built for what comes next.

APEX Finance has served Lebanese companies since 2012. We keep their books, file their taxes and NSSF, prepare their financial statements and sign their audits.

Almost all of our clients came through other clients. Our reputation rests on two things: work done right, and work done on time.

We serve more than 270 clients: corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies in more than 50 sectors, from hospitals and supermarket chains to manufacturers, schools, hotels and offshore companies, in Lebanon and abroad.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else, just contact us.

Do you work with my current ERP?

Yes. We work in CTS, Omega, SAGE and most systems used in Lebanon, or we keep the books in ours. We also build import files so nothing is typed twice.

How do I send you documents?

By email or a shared folder: scanned invoices, receipts, bank statements or ERP exports. We book them and tell you if anything is missing. WhatsApp intake is coming next.

What does it cost?

A fixed monthly fee based on your number of entries and entities. You get a written quote once we understand your needs.

Do you work with companies outside Lebanon?

Yes. Besides Lebanese companies, we serve clients in Iraq, Armenia, Cyprus and the United States, and offshore companies.
