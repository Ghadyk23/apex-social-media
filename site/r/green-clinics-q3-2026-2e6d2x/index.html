  • Revenue was flat at $144,385 for the quarter — $46,676, $49,500 and $48,209. Two opposite movements cancelled out: clinic consultations rose in every month, Willow shop sales fell 43.3%.
  • The clinic is carrying the business. Consultations grew from $33,950 to $39,530, 76.9% of revenue, on 392 visits. Online reached $17,460 in September, 44.2% of clinic revenue against 32.0% in July.
  • September was the weakest month at $19,911, down $3,051 on August, in the month with the strongest clinic revenue. The whole fall sits in Willow: net purchases $6,641 against sales $6,339.
  • Overheads are stable. Operating expenses averaged $5,933 a month and payroll $13,750, with payroll inside one percentage point of revenue across all three months.
Revenue, gross profit and result · $ 000
Willow is the whole of the decline.

Trading margin fell from 73.6% to (4.8%) across the quarter. Net purchases of $13,733 against sales of $23,645 left only $9,912 of contribution from the shop in three months, a margin of 41.9% for the quarter.

The clinic absorbed it.

Consultations added $5,580 between July and September on 18 more visits, which is why total revenue barely moved while the shop fell $4,846.

The cost base is holding.

Operating expenses and payroll together were $59,051, 40.9% of revenue, with a spread of only $1,532 between the highest and lowest month.

Revenue and costs on an accrual basis. Willow purchases are recorded as incurred, with no opening or closing stock adjustment, so the monthly Willow margin is not a true cost of sales.
